and diffusion-cover.jpg in the same directory.

Quantum-computing article series
The sources content/quantum-computing-basics.html,
content/quantum-neural-networks.html, and content/grover-search.html are
edited from the site author's handwritten notes and supplied text draft.
Corrections and explanatory additions are incorporated into the articles.
The articles link to IBM Quantum Learning, PennyLane's quantum-gradient
tutorial, and the original/review papers by Grover, Cerezo et al., and
McClean et al. No external figures or screenshots from those sources are
redistributed.

The three SVG covers, Bell/QNN circuit diagrams, and HTML/SVG interactive
figures in blog/quantum/ were created for this site. They are explanatory
illustrations and ideal-state calculations, not quantum-hardware results.
No photographed notebook pages are included in the published site.

The retained alternative quantum-series covers are original image_gen.imagegen
editorial illustrations, using a lighter interpretation of this site's diffusion-cover
style. blog/quantum/{basics,qnn,grover}-cover-v2.png retain the generated
originals; matching .jpg files are web exports. Each *-cover-v2-prompt.txt
records its complete prompt and source. The live site uses the original three
*-cover.svg files, preserved unchanged. The covers are conceptual artwork, not quantitative
plots, exact circuit diagrams, or experimental results.
