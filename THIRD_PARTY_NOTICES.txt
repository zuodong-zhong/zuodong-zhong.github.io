Diffusion-model articles and retained figures
The local articles are edited, typeset versions of the site author's public
Zhihu learning notes:
- Score-based Generative Models
  Source: https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/25390096275
  Local source: content/score-based-generative-models.html
- DDPM
  Source: https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/22870758906
  Local source: content/ddpm.html
- DDIM
  Source: https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/25355354086
  Local source: content/ddim.html

Formula, algorithm, and derivation screenshots were transcribed into text
and mathematical notation, with notation corrections described in the
articles. The DDPM and DDIM notes retain attribution to Su Jianlin's
derivations at https://spaces.ac.cn/archives/9164 and
https://spaces.ac.cn/archives/9181, respectively. Each local article also
links its original research papers in the references.

Among the newly added DDPM and DDIM articles, only the DDPM forward-process
illustration is retained as an image from the Zhihu original:
blog/ddpm/forward-process.jpg, from https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/22870758906.
The existing Score article also retains blog/score-based-generative-models/
score-field.jpg from https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/25390096275.
These source attributions do not place the articles or retained figures
under the software licenses above.

AI-generated editorial covers
The covers are original image_gen.imagegen artwork, used as editorial
illustrations rather than scientific figures or experimental results.
The new DDPM and DDIM covers have their full prompts and generation-source
records in blog/ddpm/cover-prompt.txt and blog/ddim/cover-prompt.txt.
Each directory contains cover.png (generated original) and cover.jpg
(web-ready image). The existing Score cover has corresponding records in
blog/score-based-generative-models/cover-prompt.txt, with diffusion-cover.png
and diffusion-cover.jpg in the same directory.

Quantum-computing article series
The sources content/quantum-computing-basics.html,
content/quantum-neural-networks.html, and content/grover-search.html are
edited from the site author's handwritten notes and supplied text draft.
Corrections and explanatory additions are incorporated into the articles.
The articles link to IBM Quantum Learning, PennyLane's quantum-gradient
tutorial, and the original/review papers by Grover, Cerezo et al., and
McClean et al. No external figures or screenshots from those sources are
redistributed.

The three SVG covers, Bell/QNN circuit diagrams, and HTML/SVG interactive
figures in blog/quantum/ were created for this site. They are explanatory
illustrations and ideal-state calculations, not quantum-hardware results.
No photographed notebook pages are included in the published site.
